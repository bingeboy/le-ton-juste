02
Lehle Parallel M
Ready to Purchase — ~$180 Used

Single parallel loop unit. Puts the QuadraVerb in a parallel loop — the dry signal path never touches the QuadraVerb's A/D converters. Ghost Spring stays in series after the Lehle; it's fully analog with its own Mix pot.

Buy used on Reverb.com or Music Go Round. Search "Lehle Parallel M."

Alembic FX-1 → Lehle Parallel M (QuadraVerb in loop) → Ghost Spring → MC100
03
OBEL Buffer
Planned

On-Board Effects Loop buffer — either a Waldotronics OBEL Buffer pedal or a DIY JFET build. Moves the volume pot downstream of the pedalboard effects so turning down doesn't kill reverb and delay tails. Keeps the guitar's high-impedance pickup output isolated from pedal loading.

Complements the rack parallel mixer — OBEL protects the guitar-to-preamp path, the Lehle protects the preamp-to-amp path. Jerry Garcia ran both on Tiger, Wolf, and Rosebud.

  • Waldotronics OBEL Buffer: Commercial pedal, purpose-built, drop-in
  • DIY JFET build: J201 or 2N5457 source follower — same topology as the guitar's output buffer on Irwin-built instruments
04
Mute Switch + Tuner Routing
Planned — Depends on OBEL

Passive mute switch on the pedalboard that silently splits signal to the Sabine RT-1601 rack tuner when stomped. Most cleanly implemented as part of the OBEL loop — the wiring design is straightforward once the OBEL buffer is in place.

Currently the Sabine RT-1601 sits in the rack receiving a passive split from before the Alembic FX-1. The mute footswitch design formalizes this into a clean rack-integrated solution.

05
Rack Compressor
Under Consideration — Do Not Add Yet

A rack compressor post-Alembic FX-1 would add the bloom and sustain character that gives notes a viola or horn-like swell — slow attack lets the pick transient through, medium-slow release sustains the tail. The goal is musical bloom, not squash.

Hold off until Ghost Spring and Lehle are in place and the chain is settled. Dial in the Alembic's EQ first — compressor after EQ in the decision sequence.

Alembic FX-1 → Compressor → Lehle Parallel M → Ghost Spring → MC100
