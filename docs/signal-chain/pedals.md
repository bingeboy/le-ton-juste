# Pedals — IO Thick Air & IO Old Dirt

## IO Thick Air — Dual JFET Clean Boost

**Builder:** IO Custom Guitars (Brian White, Laramie, WY)
**Type:** Dual-stage clean boost / preamp / tone enhancer

### Architecture

- Two independent JFET preamp stages, each with dedicated footswitch and gain control
- NOS Siliconix J230 JFET transistors (individually tested)
- Audiophile-grade components: non-magnetic resistors, NOS German & Russian film/foil capacitors, mil-spec silver-tinned Teflon wire
- No electrolytic capacitors (will never need replacing)
- Right channel includes an "Air" toggle switch for upper-frequency enhancement
- Circuit based on the classic Stratoblaster design, heavily refined

### Sound Character

- Adds presence, air, vibrance without compression — described as a "3-dimensional sound stage"
- No distortion from the pedal itself; can push downstream devices into overdrive
- "Dynamic grit threshold" — subtle harmonic enhancement that reacts to pick attack
- Very hi-fi, sparkly top end

### Placement

**Always first in chain** (or immediately after an OBEL buffer if present). Stage 1 at minimum gain acts as a permanent buffer/tone enhancer. Stage 2 is used as a solo/lead boost.

### Usage Notes

- Start with Stage 1 gain low (9 o'clock or minimum) as your "always on" tone sweetener
- Use the Air toggle on Stage 2 for cutting through a mix — it boosts upper frequencies without harshness
- Both stages engaged = maximum boost into the preamp. This is likely to push the Alembic into its sweet spot without overdriving it

## IO Old Dirt — Distortion

**Builder:** IO Custom Guitars
**Type:** Distortion pedal
**Status:** Very new release (late 2025). Limited public documentation.

### What's Known

- Newest pedal from IO Custom Guitars
- Name references a Grateful Dead lyric ("New Minglewood Blues")
- Designed to complement the Deadhead/Jerry tone aesthetic
- Likely a moderate-gain overdrive/distortion voiced for the Alembic/McIntosh rig

### Placement

After Thick Air, before the preamp. Standard gain stacking — Thick Air pushes the Dirt, Dirt hits the Alembic's tube input.

## Gain Staging — Thick Air + Dirt + Preamp

```
Thick Air (buffer + subtle boost) → Old Dirt (clipping) → Alembic FX-1 (tube warmth, EQ)
```

The chain is designed so:
1. Thick Air sets the base tone — sparkle, presence, 3D feel
2. Old Dirt adds controllable grit — into a clean preamp for edge-of-breakup textures
3. Alembic FX-1 provides Fender-style tube EQ and final voicing before the time-based effects
